# Changelog

What each release of Termina changed, newest first. A release's section, under a heading of
its version, is put at the top of its notes on the release page (`release.yml`).

## 0.4.4

- **Reopen a closed tab**: Shell ▸ Reopen Closed Tab, ⌘⇧T (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+T on Linux). It
  opens a new shell in the closed tab's directory, at its place on the bar, and goes back
  through the last 10 tabs closed. The old output is not brought back yet.
- **Tabs flare out at their foot**, their bottom corners curving out to meet the line under
  them.
- **Termina Dark's tabs are lighter**, so an unselected tab stands out from the window.
